# NBA, NFL and NCAAF forward market adjustments

The existing flow policy still determines the original signed adjustment, including the existing sizing mode. For new eligible observations at or after November 25, 2026 at 22:30 UTC (5:30 p.m. Eastern), v2 uses native home margin minus twice the original spread adjustment and native total minus twice the original total adjustment. Spread edge is adjusted home margin plus signed market home handicap. Total edge is adjusted total minus market total. Equal edges abstain within numerical tolerance. The score pair is derived from that same adjusted margin and total. The UI consumes these saved projections directly. There is no historical display transformation.

Model versions use the lowercase league prefix followed by `-market-tracking-2026-11-25-v2`. Identify a new decision by its row version and `market_tracking.adjustment_policy = original-adjustments-minus-2x-v1`, not the time the page was opened. The UTC decision boundary is stored in `adjustment_policy_effective_at`. The older activation and flow policy identify the original tracking system. Preserve game, forecast identity, UTC decision and published clocks, and final status when comparing rows. Use one final pregame decision per game.

In the public NBA forecast, consult `market_tracking`; on a football slate row, parse `Market Tracking JSON`. Both contain `original_spread_shadow` and `original_total_shadow`. Each stores baseline, raw_adjustment, adjustment, projection, contributions, edge, original_side, applied_side and discordant. The saved line key is home_line on spreads and line on totals. A positive side means home or Over, a negative side means away or Under, and zero means abstain. Use the original shadow projection and original_side directly for v2. Use the saved original forecast directly for v1. Do not multiply either by another factor. The capped comparator remains a separate historical sizing comparison. Existing settlement, eligibility and official pick records are unchanged.

The writer retains existing idempotency, immutable final locks, NCAAF authoritative-final guards and the positive-score check. No backfill or database migration is required. To return eligible new observations to original direction, deploy this compatible build with EZPZ_THREE_SPORT_REVERSAL_DISABLED=1. Do not roll readers back to a binary that cannot validate v2. The cap setting and the NCAAM writer remain independent.
